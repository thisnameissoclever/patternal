# MakeHuman asset notice

`makehuman-base.obj` is the MakeHuman hm08 base mesh from the official `makehumancommunity/mpfb2` repository.

`Production/Avatar` is generated from the official MakeHuman Community system asset pack. It contains the measurement-fitted hm08 display body plus fitted eyes, teeth, eyebrows, eyelashes, and bob hair. The body and accessory meshes preserve MakeHuman UV coordinates and use the pack's skin, eye, dental, brow, lash, and hair textures.

The asset header states that it was explicitly released under Creative Commons Zero in September 2020. The original copyright holders listed in the asset are Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, and Jonas Hauquier.
